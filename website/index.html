PaalalaPlease note

Paunang pagsusuri ang ChickDetect para matulungan kang makita agad ang posibleng sakit. Hindi nito pinapalitan ang pagsusuri ng beterinaryo — ipakumpirma sa kanila, lalo na kung nakakahawang sakit. Sa Pilipinas, dapat i-report sa munisipyo ang hinihinalang Newcastle Disease. ChickDetect is a preliminary check that helps you spot possible disease early. It does not replace a veterinarian's findings — have a vet confirm it, especially for a disease that spreads. In the Philippines, suspected Newcastle Disease must be reported to your local government.
